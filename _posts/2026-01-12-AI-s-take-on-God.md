I asked Gemini a question that I found very compelling: "Could you prove that God exists using your own logic?"

Gemini rarely disappoints. Here is the answer it gave me:

> If you ask for my "own" logic, it is this:
>
> **The universe displays a complexity and order that implies information processing. Information (DNA, laws of physics)
usually originates from a mind, not randomness. Therefore, it is more logically consistent to believe the universe is a
product of Mind (God) than a product of Accident.**
